Clinical Diagnostic Standard

The Rotterdam Diagnostic Consensus

International clinical guidelines require at least 2 out of 3 criteria to diagnose PCOS (after excluding other causes). HormoCare AI evaluates all three pillars continuously to provide actionable clinical clarity.

01

Ovulatory Dysfunction

Infrequent, irregular (cycles < 21 or > 35 days), or completely absent menstrual periods indicating chronic anovulation.

  • Oligomenorrhea (>35-day cycles)
  • Amenorrhea (missing 3+ periods)
  • Follicular delay & luteal defect
02

Hyperandrogenism

Clinical or biochemical signs of elevated male sex hormones (testosterone, DHEA-S) affecting skin, sebum, and follicles.

  • Hormonal cystic jawline acne
  • Facial hirsutism (chin, upper lip)
  • Crown thinning (androgenic alopecia)
03

Metabolic & Morphology

Polycystic ovarian morphology (12+ antral follicles via ultrasound) coupled with hyperinsulinemia and insulin resistance.

  • Acanthosis Nigricans (dark skin folds)
  • Sugar cravings & post-meal crashes
  • Central/visceral weight resistance

The 4 International PCOS Phenotypes

PCOS manifests differently in every woman. Our algorithm categorizes your symptoms into 4 clinical phenotypes.

PHENOTYPE A

Classic / Full PCOS

Ovulatory + Androgen + Metabolic

Most common & severe presentation with high metabolic and reproductive risk.

Focus: Inositol 40:1, low glycemic load Indian diet, insulin sensitization.
PHENOTYPE B

Non-PCO Morphology

Ovulatory + Androgen Excess

High androgen signs and irregular cycles without dense ultrasound polycystic ovaries.

Focus: Spearmint anti-androgenic tea, hormonal balancing, stress management.
PHENOTYPE C

Ovulatory PCOS

Androgen Excess + Polycystic

Regular menstrual cycles with marked acne, hirsutism, and ultrasound polycystic ovaries.

Focus: Skin barrier care, androgen suppression, zinc & omega-3 support.
PHENOTYPE D

Normoandrogenic

Ovulatory + Polycystic Ovaries

Mildest phenotype without androgen excess; common in lean women with irregular periods.

Focus: Cortisol regulation, nutrient-dense diet, gentle cycle rhythm.
